FAQ ❓

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers about CogniFocus, how it works, permissions, availability, and Pro features.

✨

What is CogniFocus?

CogniFocus is an Android-first focus companion that combines a reliable timer with a Distraction Shield and behavioral nudges. It helps you form resilient focus habits by detecting app switching, protecting sessions, and offering recovery actions and progress tracking.

⏱

How is it different from a normal timer?

Traditional timers count time but don't help you stay on task when distractions occur. CogniFocus monitors foreground apps and uses a Shield to intervene, combined with reminders, planned sessions, and progress features to reinforce focused behavior.

📵

What happens if I open TikTok or Instagram?

When you open a selected distracting app during a protected session, the Distraction Shield can interrupt the app and present a blocking or recovery screen. You can return to the session or continue; the app preserves session state so you don't lose progress unintentionally.

🔒

Why does it need Android permissions?

CogniFocus requests Usage Access to detect which app is in the foreground and optionally Display Over Other Apps to present the Shield. These permissions let the app protect sessions without reading private messages or accessing app content.

🍎

Is CogniFocus available on iOS?

Not at this time. The team plans iOS support but the Android app is the current focus. Join the iOS waitlist via the site to receive updates when an iOS release is available.

💎

What does Pro add?

Pro expands Shield capacity, enables Manual Block, adds Planned Sessions and persistent reminders, unlocks additional customization, and provides priority restore support for paying users.
