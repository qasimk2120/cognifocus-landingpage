CogniFocus – Account & Data Deletion

Below are the available options to delete your account and associated data.

Introduction

Users may delete their account and associated data. Choose the in-app option for immediate removal, or send a manual request via email.

Option 1 (In-app deletion)

Steps to delete your account from within the app:

  • Open CogniFocus
  • Go to Settings
  • Tap "Delete Account"

Option 2 (Manual request)

If you prefer a manual request, email us at:

getcognifocus@gmail.com

Data deleted

When you delete your account we remove the following:

  • Account information (Google account if linked)
  • Focus sessions and stats
  • Streaks and progress
  • App preferences

Data retention

Some data may be retained for up to 30 days for security or legal reasons.

Anonymous users

  • Anonymous users do not have a permanent account
  • Their data is stored locally
  • Data is deleted when the app is uninstalled
