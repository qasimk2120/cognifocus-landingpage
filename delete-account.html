Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your CogniFocus account from Settings. This page explains what is deleted, what may remain with subscription providers, and how to get help

🗑

Delete From Settings

The recommended way to delete your account is directly inside CogniFocus

  1. Open CogniFocus.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt.
💾

What Gets Deleted

Account deletion removes synced profile and stats data that can be deleted from the client, clears local app data, and deletes the Firebase account

  • Firebase account information linked to CogniFocus
  • Synced profile settings and onboarding state
  • Daily stats, streak, progress, and planned session data
  • Local app state, preferences, selected blocked apps, and session state
💳

Subscriptions Are Separate

Deleting your CogniFocus account does not cancel a Google Play subscription

Subscriptions must be managed or cancelled through Google Play. Some purchase records may remain with Google Play or RevenueCat as required for subscription management, restoration, compliance, fraud prevention, tax, legal, or platform requirements

📱

Local Data

Locally stored data may also be removed when you clear app data or uninstall CogniFocus

Local data may include app state and preferences, theme, motion, accessibility and color settings, selected blocked apps, focus session state, planned sessions, reminder state, and some progress or session-related data

📧

Need Help?

For account or privacy requests, use the support option in CogniFocus Settings. You can also contact us by email
