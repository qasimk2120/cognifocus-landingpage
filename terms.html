Permissions

Some Android permissions may be requested to enable Shield and related app controls.

Usage Access lets Shield detect which apps you open so it can catch distractions. It does not read messages or in-app content.

Display Over Other Apps lets CogniFocus show a blocking screen over distracting apps when Shield or Manual Block needs to interrupt you.

These permissions are optional, but Shield and Manual Block may not work correctly without them. You can change or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.
