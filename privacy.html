Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for CogniFocus.

Last updated: April 18, 2026. CogniFocus helps you manage focus sessions, block distracting apps, track progress, use character-based motivation, and manage premium features.

💻

Data Stored On Your Device

CogniFocus stores some data locally on your device so core app features can work.

  • App state and preferences
  • Theme, motion, accessibility, and color settings
  • Selected blocked apps
  • Focus session state and planned sessions
  • Nudge cooldowns and reminder state
  • Some progress and session-related data

Locally stored data may be removed when you clear app data, delete your account from Settings, or uninstall the app.

🔑

Accounts And Authentication

CogniFocus uses Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous user account by default.

If Google linking is added or enabled later, Google sign-in will link to your existing anonymous account instead of replacing it. This helps protect your progress and premium access across reinstalls or devices.

We may store your Firebase user ID to connect app data to your account.

🗂

Data Collected And Stored

CogniFocus may store user-linked app data in Firebase Firestore.

  • Firebase user ID, profile settings, and onboarding state
  • Platform, app version, timestamps, and premium/subscription status fields
  • Daily stats, streak and progress data, and planned session data
  • Support request records and account deletion records

This data is used to operate app features, maintain progress, support syncing where available, process and reflect subscription status, provide support, improve reliability, and prevent abuse.

✉

Support Requests

When you contact support, CogniFocus may store support details.

  • The message you submit, optional name, and optional email address
  • Firebase user ID, app version, platform, and session/support context
  • Recent app actions/logs and recent error details

This information is used to reply to you, debug issues, rate-limit abuse, and improve app reliability. Support free-text is not used for analytics.

🛡

Permissions

CogniFocus may request Android permissions to enable Shield and app-blocking features.

Usage Access: CogniFocus uses Usage Access to detect which apps you open so Shield can catch distractions. It does not read messages or in-app content.

Display Over Other Apps: CogniFocus uses Overlay permission to show a blocking screen over distracting apps when Shield or Manual Block needs to interrupt you.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings. Some features may not work correctly without them.

💳

Subscriptions And Purchases

CogniFocus uses RevenueCat and Google Play to manage subscription products, entitlement status, purchase restoration, and subscription management.

CogniFocus may receive subscription-related information such as whether your account has active Pro access. Payment details are handled by Google Play and are not stored by CogniFocus.

Anonymous users can purchase Pro. Linking Google later helps protect restore access across reinstalls and devices.

📊

Analytics And Diagnostics

CogniFocus uses Firebase services to understand app usage and improve reliability.

Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Performance Monitoring may be used to detect crashes, diagnose performance issues, and improve reliability.

Analytics and diagnostics may include app events, device/platform information, crash logs, performance traces, and feature usage signals.

🔗

Third-Party Services

CogniFocus uses trusted providers to operate app functionality.

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring
  • RevenueCat
  • Google Play billing/subscription services

These providers process data on our behalf to provide authentication, cloud storage, diagnostics, analytics, purchase handling, and app functionality. CogniFocus does not sell your personal data.

🌐

Cookies and similar technologies

CogniFocus uses Microsoft Clarity on this website to understand page visits and improve the site experience. We may also use essential browser storage for basic site functionality or preferences, such as remembering interface choices. CogniFocus does not sell your personal data, and we do not use third-party advertising cookies.

🗑

Data Retention And Deletion

CogniFocus keeps account, progress, support, and subscription status data while needed to operate the app, provide support, comply with platform requirements, or until deleted according to app controls and operational needs.

You can delete your CogniFocus account from Settings. This removes synced profile and stats data that can be deleted from the client, clears local app data, and deletes the Firebase account.

Deleting your CogniFocus account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Subscriptions must be managed or cancelled through Google Play.

Some purchase records may remain managed by Google Play and RevenueCat for subscription management, restore, fraud-prevention, tax, legal, or platform compliance purposes.

⚙

Your Controls

You can manage your permissions, account, and app data.

  • Revoke optional permissions in device settings
  • Stop using the app at any time
  • Uninstall the app to remove local data
  • Delete your account from Settings
  • Contact support for access or deletion help
📧

Contact

For privacy questions or support, use the support option in CogniFocus Settings.
