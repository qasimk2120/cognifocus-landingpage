Why I built CogniFocus

I built CogniFocus because normal timers never worked for me.

I could start a focus session and still end up switching apps without noticing. The timer kept running, but the time was already wasted.

So I wanted focus to feel less passive - more like a companion keeping an eye on the session and pulling you back when you drift.

That became CogniFocus: a timer, a blocker, streaks, recovery nudges, and a Goblin that reacts the moment your focus slips.

What is CogniFocus? +
A compact Android app that combines a focus timer with a Distraction Shield, progress tracking, and gentle recovery nudges.
How is it different from a normal timer? +
Unlike a plain timer, CogniFocus detects distraction and actively helps return you to your session.
What happens if I open TikTok or Instagram? +
During protected sessions the Distraction Shield can interrupt selected apps and encourage returning to focus.
Why does it need Android permissions? +
Permissions let the app detect foreground apps and show the Shield when needed; it does not read private messages.
Is CogniFocus available on iOS? +
Not yet — Android is supported now. Sign up for iOS notifications via the iOS waitlist.
What does Pro add? +
Pro unlocks stronger Shield capacity, Manual Block, planned sessions, reminders and restore support.
